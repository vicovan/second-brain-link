# Email playbook

## The cold email — five lines, one ask

```
Subject: <company one-liner in ≤ 8 words> — <stage> raise

Hi <first name>,

<Why you: one sentence tied to a ✅ claim — their thesis page, a portfolio company, a piece they wrote.>
<What: the one-liner from company.md.>
<Proof: one or two checkable facts from company.md — shipped things, not adjectives.>
<Who: one line from founder.md.>
<Ask: "Would you be open to a 20-minute call next week?" — or the fund's own stated process.>

<deck URL> · <site>
<name>
```

- Under 120 words. No attachments. No "I know you're busy".
- The hook names something specific and verifiable. "I love your portfolio" is not a hook.
- One investor per email. Never a BCC, never a template with the name swapped and nothing else.
- Proof is facts the founder has published; anything on the do-not-claim list is refused by the linter.

## The follow-up — shorter, with one new fact

```
Subject: Re: <original subject>

Hi <first name> — one update since my note: <a new, checkable fact>. Happy to send more, or to
leave it here if the timing isn't right.
```

Cadence: first follow-up after `followup_days` (default 7), second after 14 more days. After two
unanswered follow-ups, propose marking the target `passed`.

## The intro request — to the connection, not the investor

```
Subject: Intro to <partner> at <fund>?

Hi <first name>,
I'm raising for <company> and <fund> is one of the few that <✅ thesis claim>. I saw you <how the
brain shows they are connected — only if it does>. Would you be comfortable introducing me to
<partner>? A forwardable note is below — edit freely.

---
<three-line blurb: what, proof, ask>
```

## Subject lines that survive a busy inbox
- `<Product in 6 words> — pre-seed` · `<Thesis phrase from their site> — built it` ·
  `Intro: <company> (<one checkable fact>)`

## Never
- Invented familiarity ("as we discussed"), invented urgency ("closing Friday") or invented social
  proof ("other funds are in") — none of it traces to the profile, so none of it passes the gate.
