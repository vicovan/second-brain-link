# stories.md — template

Five short stories every application asks for in some form. Each is written once, from real events
the founder confirms, and trimmed per form to its limit.

```markdown
---
type: fundraising-profile
title: Stories
---

## Origin — why this company
While running settlement at a card processor, reconciliation across currencies took a week per
marketplace client. The fix was an API, and nobody sold it to small marketplaces.

## Why now
Instant-payment rails became mandatory for banks in the region, so the plumbing finally exists.

## Why you
Built settlement systems at scale before; the pilot integrations took days, not months.

## The hardest thing so far
Losing the first design partner to an acquisition — and what changed afterwards.

## Unfair advantage
Access to the rails through a prior employer's sandbox programme (public, not a partnership).
```
