# Dining and activity sources

| Site | Use it for | Link |
|---|---|---|
| Google Maps | coordinates, opening hours, the neighbourhood | `https://www.google.com/maps/search/<query>+near+<place>` |
| Tripadvisor | a second opinion on food | `https://www.tripadvisor.com/Search?q=<query>` |
| GetYourGuide | tours and tickets | `https://www.getyourguide.com/s/?q=<city>` |
| Viator | tours | `https://www.viator.com/searchResults/all?text=<city>` |

## Matching taste

- Match the *reason* they loved a place, not only its category: "tiny counter, no laptops"
  rules out a laptop-friendly chain even though both are cafés.
- A high public rating is not their taste. A ★4.8 buffet is still a buffet if they rated one ★2.
- Prefer places near the day's route (under ~10 minutes' walk from it) over a detour.
