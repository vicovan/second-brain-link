# Stay sources

| Site | Pre-filled link |
|---|---|
| Google Hotels | `https://www.google.com/travel/hotels/<city>?q=hotels%20in%20<city>&dates=<YYYY-MM-DD>,<YYYY-MM-DD>&guests=<n>` (or search "hotels in <city>" on google.com/travel/hotels and set the dates) — prices across sites, real availability, the map |
| Booking.com | `https://www.booking.com/searchresults.html?ss=<city>&checkin=<YYYY-MM-DD>&checkout=<YYYY-MM-DD>&group_adults=<n>` |
| Agoda | `https://www.agoda.com/search?city=<city>&checkIn=<YYYY-MM-DD>&los=<nights>&adults=<n>` (city may need to be chosen on the page) |
| Airbnb | `https://www.airbnb.com/s/<city>/homes?checkin=<YYYY-MM-DD>&checkout=<YYYY-MM-DD>&adults=<n>` |

## Traps

- Prices shown per night vs total, and before vs after taxes and cleaning fees — record which.
- "Only 1 left" and countdown banners are sales pressure, not facts. Do not repeat them.
- A listing's map pin may be approximate (Airbnb) — say so when judging walking distance.
- A stopover night: check the check-in time against the arrival; a late arrival may need
  "arriving after midnight" noted, or the night before booked.
