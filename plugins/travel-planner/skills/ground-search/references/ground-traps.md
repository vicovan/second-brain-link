# Ground transport — the traps worth checking before recommending anything

## Car hire
- **One-way fees** — dropping the car in another city can cost more than the rental; across a
  border, far more. Quote the one-way total, not the daily rate.
- **Cross-border restrictions** — many rentals forbid taking the car into some countries, or
  charge for it. Check before routing a drive across a border.
- **Driver age** — under 25 (sometimes under 21) and over ~70 surcharges or refusals.
- **Licence** — some countries require an International Driving Permit alongside a
  national licence. Say "check whether you need one"; never say one is not needed.
- **City centres** — low-emission zones, limited-traffic zones and parking often make a car a
  liability in the old town. A car for the countryside, the train for the city.
- **Fuel and deposit** — full-to-full vs pre-paid fuel; the card hold on pick-up.

## Rail
- Advance fares vs flexible fares — note which was quoted.
- Seat reservations are mandatory on some high-speed trains even with a pass.
- Airport ↔ city trains are the cheapest part of a stopover night; include them.

## Ferries and buses
- Ferries: vehicle vs foot-passenger fares differ hugely; seasonal timetables.
- Long-distance buses: often cheapest; stations can be far from the centre.
