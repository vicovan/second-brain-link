> Behavioural answers, written once from real events and reused — on forms that ask "tell us about
> a time…", and in interview prep. Every number in a story must also appear in `profile.md`;
> anything the person cannot confirm is marked `(unconfirmed)` and is never used on a form.

# Story bank

## S1 · <short title>
- **Tags:** leadership · conflict · failure · ownership · scale · delivery · hiring · ambiguity
- **Situation:** where, when, what was at stake — one or two sentences.
- **Task:** what this person specifically had to do.
- **Action:** what they did, in the first person, concretely. The part interviewers probe.
- **Result:** the outcome, with the profile's own numbers.
- **Reflection:** what they would do differently, or what it taught — the line that signals seniority.
- **Source:** the `profile.md` role this comes from.

Aim for eight: leadership, a hard decision, a conflict, a failure, ownership beyond the brief,
growing a team, delivering under a constraint, and adapting to a change of direction.
