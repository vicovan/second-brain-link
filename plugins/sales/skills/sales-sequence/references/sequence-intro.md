# Intro — ask the connector

The first message goes to the CONNECTOR (the person you know), never cold to the target. Add the
connector to the account as a contact whose role reads "connector", with their own address.
Make the ask easy to forward: a two-line blurb they can paste.

| n | channel | wait_days | kind | requires | brief |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | email | 0 | first | | to the connector: why this person, why now, a forwardable two-line blurb, an easy "no" |
| 2 | email | 5 | follow-up | | to the connector: one line, no pressure |
