# Cold — signal-led

For an account the scout found on the public web. Every first line cites the signal (the posting,
the round, the post they wrote), with its link, so the message could only have been written to them.

| n | channel | wait_days | kind | requires | brief |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | email | 0 | first | | the signal in their words; the one problem it implies; one proof point; one question |
| 2 | linkedin_invite | 2 | connect | social | under 300 characters; name the signal; no pitch |
| 3 | email | 4 | follow-up | | a different angle: a short example of the outcome, from the offer profile |
| 4 | form | 6 | form | | only when no address was published: the company's own contact form, the same message, shorter |
| 5 | email | 8 | breakup | | close the loop; ask who owns this if not them |
