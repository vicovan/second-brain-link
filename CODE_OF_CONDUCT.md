# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in the
Second Brain Link community a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Our standards

We adopt the **[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)**
as the standard of behavior for this project. In short:

- Be respectful, considerate, and collaborative.
- Welcome newcomers and assume good faith.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- No personal attacks, harassment, or discriminatory language or conduct.

The full text — including the complete list of expected and unacceptable behaviors,
enforcement responsibilities, and enforcement guidelines — is available at the link
above and applies to this project in full.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (issues, pull requests,
discussions, and any other community channel) and when an individual is officially
representing the project in public spaces.

## Reporting & enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported
to the project maintainer at **adrian@vicovan.com**. All reports will be reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy and security will be
respected. Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this Code of Conduct in good
faith may face temporary or permanent repercussions as determined by the project's
leadership.
