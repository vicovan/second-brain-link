# Instagram export

Drop your unzipped **"Download your information"** export here (or pass the `.zip`).
**Choose JSON.** Contents are git-ignored. Builds profile + follows/followers →
people, posts & topics → voice/interests, saved locations → `85-places/`, and
message **signal only** (per-person frequency/recency — bodies are never read).

Download: Instagram → *Accounts Center* → *Your information and permissions* →
*Download your information* → **JSON**.
