# Facebook export

Drop your unzipped **"Download your information"** export here (or pass the `.zip`).
**Choose format: JSON** when you request it. Contents are git-ignored. Builds
friends/followers → people, posts/comments/reactions → voice, liked pages →
interests, ad-interests/advertisers/off-Meta → the `50-mirror/` layer, check-ins
and locations → `85-places/`, event invitations → events, and your profile.

Download: Facebook → *Settings* → *Your information* → *Download your information* →
format **JSON**.
