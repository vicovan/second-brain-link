# LinkedIn export

Drop your unzipped **LinkedIn data archive** here (or pass the `.zip`). Contents
are git-ignored. The richest source — profile, connections, companies,
recommendations, posts, job applications, and the algorithm's inferences about you.

Download: LinkedIn → **Me** → *Settings & Privacy* → *Data Privacy* → *Get a copy
of your data* → **Download larger data archive** (the full one — can take up to 24h;
connections-only is faster but far less complete).
